# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, and technical interviewers evaluating Nguyen Phu Trieu for entry-level DevOps and cloud engineering opportunities. They need to understand his focus, evidence of hands-on work, and contact path quickly.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Nguyen Phu Trieu's DevOps profile, work experience, education, technical toolkit, selected projects, and engineering writing. Success means a visitor can identify his strengths, inspect concrete work, and contact him without friction.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects backend engineering foundations to practical cloud operations through real projects involving AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, GitOps, CI/CD, observability, security, and automation.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically scan the site on desktop or mobile from a resume, GitHub profile, or shared link. They may move between About, Projects, Blog, source repositories, and contact links while comparing candidates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router application deployed on Vercel.
- English and Vietnamese content with a persistent language preference.
- Light and dark themes with a persistent theme preference.
- Responsive navigation and layouts.
- Static portfolio content; no CMS or authenticated application state.
- The canonical public domain is `https://nguyen-phu-trieu-portfolio.vercel.app/`.

## Brand Commitments

- The identity is Nguyen Phu Trieu, DevOps Engineer.
- Vietnamese text must render correctly in every supported font and layout.
- A Canva-inspired cyan-to-blue-to-purple gradient is the principal color relationship, used on focal sheets, primary actions, and page-level headings only.
- The tone is direct, technically credible, and early-career without inflated claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Work experience at XBrain and Techhaus Vietnam in `app/page.tsx`.
- Education, IELTS certification, and technical toolkit in `app/page.tsx`.
- Six selected projects with repository links in `app/projects/projects.ts`.
- One published GitOps article in `app/blog/posts.ts`.
- GitHub, LinkedIn, Facebook, and email contact links in shared site components.
- No testimonials, client logos, performance benchmarks, or commercial claims are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Lead with concrete engineering evidence.
- Make scanning fast while preserving enough detail for technical evaluation.
- Keep navigation, language, theme, repositories, and contact actions obvious.
- Treat bilingual readability and responsive behavior as core functionality.
- Prefer a small maintainable component system over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must remain keyboard navigable, respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve visible focus states, and maintain readable contrast in both themes.
