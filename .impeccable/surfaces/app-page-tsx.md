# Portfolio surface brief

- Scope: About, Projects, Blog index, article, and shared navigation/footer.
- Visitor mode: Experience on portfolio routes; Read on article routes.
- Audience: recruiters, hiring managers, and technical interviewers evaluating an entry-level DevOps candidate.
- Job: understand focus and credibility quickly, inspect projects or writing, then contact or open source repositories.
- Evidence: real experience, education, toolkit, six repositories, and one GitOps article. No invented metrics.
- Direction: infrastructure change-set folio—cool paper surfaces, ink-black typography, cobalt and ultraviolet document fields, registration details, and compact technical rules.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport pairs the career statement with a deployment manifest containing only verified capabilities.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/home-change-set-approved.png`.
- Component grammar: square and lightly clipped sheets; 1px ink rules; no shadows except a soft layered-paper lift; buttons are rectangular labels; content rows replace repeated cards.
- Type ramp: controlled grotesk display, compact metadata, body copy at a comfortable reading measure; Vietnamese diacritics are non-negotiable.
- Responsive behavior: desktop sheets stack into a single linear reading order on mobile; controls remain explicit and keyboard accessible.
- Asset inventory: all core content, rules, marks, and fields are semantic HTML/CSS; the approved comp is reference-only and ships no raster UI.
